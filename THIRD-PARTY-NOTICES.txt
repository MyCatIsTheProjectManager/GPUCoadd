3. METHODS

Star matching uses triangle asterisms, as described in:
    M. Beroiz, J. B. Cabral, B. Sanchez, "Astroalign: A Python module for astronomical image
    registration", Astronomy and Computing 32, 100384 (2020).

Other published methods GPUCoadd implements:
    RANSAC: M. A. Fischler, R. C. Bolles, Communications of the ACM 24(6), 1981.
    Similarity fit: S. Umeyama, IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence
    13(4), 1991.
    Cubic convolution: R. Keys, IEEE Transactions on Acoustics, Speech, and Signal Processing
    29(6), 1981.
    Biweight midvariance: T. C. Beers, K. Flynn, K. Gebhardt, The Astronomical Journal 100, 1990.
    Smootherstep feathering: K. Perlin, "Improving Noise", SIGGRAPH 2002.
